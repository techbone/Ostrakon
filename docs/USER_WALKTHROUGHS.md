## Tasks and critical questions

1. Choose the recorded mainnet dataset. Identify its block and collection date.
   Is this a live scan? Does “declared complete” describe the whole chain?
2. Open Transactions and inspect its eligible Ironwood exit. Identify the 0.25 ZEC
   public payout, 0.00015 ZEC fee and 0.25015 ZEC net outflow. Does eligibility prove
   that it belongs to a particular earlier payment?
3. Filter “No public payout.” Explain why two records are excluded. Could they
   still contain hidden payments? Can an excluded record be called private or safe?
4. Combine that reason with an ID/address filter, switch between Timeline and
   Table, then restore all seven records. Explain why summary counts and visible
   filtered counts can differ. What order do same-block records appear in?
5. Choose the synthetic ambiguous example and inspect its candidate evidence.
   Explain why both matching exits remain possibilities. Does zero matching
   evidence in another snapshot guarantee anonymity?

Critical understanding: recorded versus live; bounded versus full-chain coverage;
approximate block time versus payment order; public net balance versus hidden note
value; eligible/excluded versus linked/private; candidate versus proven ownership.
